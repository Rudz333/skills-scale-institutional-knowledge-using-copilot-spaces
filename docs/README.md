## 🎯 Overview of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a structured five-phase lifecycle designed to deliver customer value through iterative, data-informed decision-making. Each phase has clear objectives, deliverables, and success criteria that ensure alignment across teams.

### The Five-Phase Lifecycle

#### 1. **Initiation** - Validate Business Need & Align Stakeholders
During the Initiation phase, we validate that proposed work aligns with business strategy and customer needs. Key activities include:
- Defining the business case and expected outcomes
- Identifying key stakeholders and their concerns
- Creating a one-pager that articulates the problem, proposed solution, and success metrics
- Securing executive sponsorship and approval to proceed

**Success Criteria**: Clear business justification, stakeholder alignment, and documented approval to move to Planning.

#### 2. **Planning** - Turn Approved Initiatives into Actionable Plans
Once approved, the Planning phase translates strategic intent into concrete work plans. This includes:
- Breaking work into shippable increments and sprints
- Estimating scope, effort, and resource requirements
- Defining acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Sequencing dependencies and identifying critical paths
- Establishing success metrics and KPIs for the project

**Success Criteria**: Comprehensive project plan, resource commitments, and clear release timeline understood by all stakeholders.

#### 3. **Execution** - Build, Test, and Track Progress
The Execution phase is where the work happens. Teams deliver increments according to the plan, with regular monitoring and adjustment:
- Conducting daily standups to identify blockers and maintain momentum
- Tracking progress against sprint goals and project milestones
- Managing scope changes and risks as they emerge
- Maintaining continuous integration and quality assurance
- Communicating status regularly to stakeholders

**Success Criteria**: Increments delivered on schedule, quality standards met, and stakeholders informed of progress and any issues.

#### 4. **Release & Deployment** - Standardized Go-Live Processes
Before any work reaches customers, it must pass Release & Deployment checks:
- Pre-release verification (functionality, performance, security)
- Production readiness assessments
- Coordinated deployment with rollback plans
- Monitoring and support during launch
- Production validation post-deployment

**Success Criteria**: Successful production deployment with no critical incidents, and confirmed customer access to new capabilities.

#### 5. **Close & Improve** - Capture Learnings & Iterate
After release, we close out the project and capture insights for continuous improvement:
- Conducting retrospectives to discuss what went well and what to improve
- Documenting lessons learned and best practices
- Measuring actual results against success criteria
- Identifying process improvements and capability gaps
- Celebrating team achievements

**Success Criteria**: Documented retrospective outcomes, confirmed learnings captured, and action items for process improvements queued.

## 💡 Key Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments to enable feedback and course correction
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead with explicit decision authority
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence, not assumptions
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning from failures, and continuous improvement
- **Transparency**: Keep stakeholders informed with regular, honest communication about progress and challenges
